LiveAs Technologies · R&D

IntraLive — Multi-Branch News Streaming Platform

Proposed, developed, and deployed an office-wide news streaming platform designed for organizations with multiple branches or offices, allowing a head office to continuously broadcast news to screens across all locations. The platform supports permanent and temporary news with priority-based scheduling, configurable display counts, and individual display timers. Temporary news is automatically removed after its configured number of appearances, while permanent news continues through subsequent cycles. Implemented a flash-news mechanism that dynamically interrupts the existing queue, displays urgent content immediately, and presents it as a breaking news item alongside the regular broadcast flow.

LiveAs Technologies · R&D

Team & Customer Interacting Module (DCIM)

Proposed, developed, and deployed a project-linked communication platform to address communication gaps between clients, sales teams, managers, and developers. When a project is registered, a dedicated group conversation is created and linked to the client's registered mobile number, allowing the client to securely access the project chat. Sales executives, team managers, and assigned developers can participate in the same conversation, with support for multimedia sharing and configurable chat availability. The team can enable or disable communication based on the project lifecycle, including restricting new messages after project delivery.
